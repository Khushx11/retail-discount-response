Evaluation and limitations

The test period starts on 6 May 2019 and was reused during development. Models forecast observed unit sales, which can be constrained by stock. They assume the target-week discount is available at prediction time. Changing the supplied markdown measures model sensitivity. Causal sales uplift and profit remain unestimated.
